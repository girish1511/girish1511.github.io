UniDAC generalizes metric depth across perspective, fisheye, and 360° cameras.

Abstract

We propose UniDAC, a unified framework for metric depth estimation across diverse camera models. By decoupling relative depth and scale estimation, our method generalizes across camera domains, including fisheye and equirectangular projections.
